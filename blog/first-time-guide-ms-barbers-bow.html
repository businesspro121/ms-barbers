A First-Timer's Guide to Visiting MS Barbers

By MS Barbers · 1 September 2026 · 3 min read

New to the shop? Here's everything worth knowing before your first visit — where we are, how walk-ins work, and how to make sure you leave with exactly the cut you asked for.

Where to find us

MS Barbers is at 458 Roman Rd, Bow, London E3 5LU, close to Bow Road. Look for the round black shop sign with the barber silhouette and scissors above the door. If you're driving or getting public transport, search "MS Barbers, Roman Road" in Google or Apple Maps and it'll take you straight there.

Walk-ins vs calling ahead

Walk-ins are always welcome. If you'd rather not wait, or you're coming at a busy time, give us a call on 07482 936667 to check how busy it is before you head over.

What to expect during your visit

Every cut — from a straightforward trim to a full skin fade — finishes with a traditional hot towel and clean-up, which is a small thing but makes a real difference to how the cut looks and feels leaving the chair. If you're after more than a haircut, beard shaping and hot towel work are available too.

Bringing kids or a first-time haircut

The team is used to working patiently with younger clients, nervous first haircuts, and clients with additional needs — taking the time needed rather than rushing through it. If it's your child's first proper haircut, it's worth mentioning that when you sit down so the barber can pace things accordingly.

How to describe the cut you want

The clearest way to get the result you're after is to combine three things: a reference photo, the fade type (skin fade or taper — see our guide to the difference), and the length you want left on top. "Short back and sides" means something different to everyone, so specifics help.

A reference photo is the most reliable way to communicate a cut, alongside naming the fade type and the length you want left on top.
