How Often Should You Get a Haircut?

By MS Barbers · 1 September 2026 · 4 min read

It's one of the most common questions we get asked in the chair: "how long can I leave it before I need to come back?" The honest answer is that it depends far more on your style than on how fast your hair grows — a tight fade and a longer, textured cut are on completely different clocks.

Skin fades and short tapers: every 2–3 weeks

A skin fade looks sharpest in the first few days after a cut, when the blend from skin to hair is crisp. Because that blend line sits in an area with very short hair, even a small amount of regrowth softens the contrast and makes the fade look "grown out" long before the length on top becomes a problem. Most clients keeping a skin fade or a low bald fade come back every 2 to 3 weeks to keep that line sharp.

Longer or textured cuts: every 4–6 weeks

If you're wearing more length on top with a scissor cut or a soft taper rather than a hard fade, you have more room to breathe. Growth is far less visible without a tight blend to show it off, so most people come back every 4 to 6 weeks — mainly to maintain the shape, clean up the neckline, and deal with split ends before they show.

Beard maintenance

A shaped beard follows a similar logic to a fade: the cheek line and neckline grow in fastest and are what make a beard look untidy first, well before the length of the beard itself changes much. Most clients getting a beard trim and shape-up come in every 2 to 4 weeks alongside their haircut.

Signs it's time to book

  • The fade line has softened and lost its contrast
  • Hair is starting to cover your ears or collar
  • The shape at the front or crown has lost its definition
  • Your beard's cheek and neck lines have grown fuzzy

If in doubt, a quick rule that works for most people: book your next appointment on the way out of your current one. It keeps you on a rhythm instead of waiting until a cut has fully grown out.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get a skin fade cut?

Every 2 to 3 weeks. A skin fade grows out fastest at the blend line, so it starts losing its sharp contrast well before the top length gets noticeably long.

How often should I get a haircut if I have longer or textured hair?

Every 4 to 6 weeks. Without a tight fade, growth is far less noticeable, so you're trimming mainly to keep the shape and remove split ends.

How often should I trim my beard?

Every 2 to 4 weeks for a shaped beard, depending on how fast it grows and how sharp you like the lines around the cheeks and neck.

What are the signs it's time for a haircut?

The fade line softening and losing contrast, hair starting to cover the ears or collar, and the shape at the front losing its definition are the three clearest signs.
